Company rules

  • To ensure all companies remain a fair and positive environment in Transport Tycoon we have laid out specific rules for the members and management of companies.
  1. You must be minimum level 30 Strength to join any company.
  2. When you leave a company you must wait 7 days from the time you leave before you can join another company.
  3. Company vs company drama, trash talking, and bullying is prohibited.
  4. Company vehicles must only be used for company work or leisure.
  5. No exploiting of any kind, if you find an exploit within your company it must be reported immediately and not shared.
  6. Companies may hire and fire whoever they want at anytime for breaking these rules or clearly laid out rules for the specific company but.....
  7. Companies and their management are expected to be fair and respectful at all times, bullying by companies and management will not be tolerated.

If you have evidence of someone breaking these company rules, or feel a company/CEO/manager has bullied or treated you unfairly, contact Stormm_29#0029 through Discord.

Useful information

Leaving / Joining Companies

Before you try to join a company, be sure to have level 30 strength and other requirements (specific to each company) listed above.

When you leave a company you will get a 7-day cooldown, if you have a cooldown you can't join another company.

If a company gets removed from the game you will not get a 7-day cooldown.
