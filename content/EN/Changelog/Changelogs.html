August 12th 2025

Hey! Here's a quick rundown of the new changes on the server:

Factions

  • Removed the "Leave Faction" button in the President Menu.
  • Faction President can now kick themselves via the member management screen instead.
  • Enabled the Public Listing system.
  • Implemented a capacity system.
  • All factions have a capacity of 50 members by default.
  • Faction President can expand the member capacity by 50 members for 1 billion faction credits.
  • Faction capacity cannot exceed 500 members.
  • Improved public list to only contain factions that are not full and can afford to onboard new members.
  • Players cannot join full factions, even when manually invited.
  • Added the option to set a Webhook URL where faction logs are sent.
  • Webhooks receive events such as new members, members leaving, perk purchases and more.
  • Members already in a faction are not affected by the capacity, although new members cannot join, and members cannot re-join if the faction is full.
  • Joining a faction via public listing costs the faction credits, if the faction cannot afford the entry cost the faction will no longer appear in the list.
  • Full factions do not appear in the public list.

Forklift Job

  • Increased the range for picking up pallets.

Summer Titles

  • Updated colors on some of the Titles.

RTS

  • Fixed some invalid vehicle entries.

January 17th 2025

ATC / Runway Utilization Detection System (RUDS)

  • We have implemented a new system, the Runway Utilization Detection System (RUDS)
  • The goal of RUDS is to ensure players utilize the air infrastrucure we have properly.
  • RUDS is integrated with the ATC system and will reward/penalize players depending on their flight performance.
  • Landing or taking off without ATC clearance counts as a RUDS violation
  • Landing or taking off anywhere that is not a marked runway counts as a RUDS violation
  • Proper (clean) takeoff manouvers are rewarded based on your current job and level
  • Proper (clean) landing manouvers are rewarded based on your current job and level
  • Cargo Pilot pickup and dropoff is restricted while you have an active violation
  • Airline Pilot rewards are lowered if you violate RUDS during your mission
  • VTOL aircraft, seaplanes and some special aircraft may be exempt from getting violations due to their different landing and takeoff behaviors
