Fishing is a calming job that takes you away from the hustle and bustle that is the Tycoon life. It lets you have thoughts about your next adventure or next lavish vehicle purchase and earn money while doing it! Does anyone smell fish?

Getting Started with Traditional Fishing

The speed of the quick-time event fishing bar is related to your FPS. If the bar is moving too fast, try enabling Vsync/Gsync/Freesync and lowering your monitor's refresh rate.

To start fishing, you need to navigate to any Job Center  (except for Life Invader) and select the Fisher job. Navigate to the Boat Shop  that’s located at the Heliport, SW of Los Santos, and purchase a boat for your ventures. If you have a boat, navigate to the Watercraft Garage and you can spawn it there.

Note: A boat is not required to get started, as the river and lakes can be accessed by land.

Once you’ve got a boat, you need to navigate to one of the many Fishing Zones, which are marked on the map by red fishhooks . When starting out, you are only able to catch certain fish. The best location to start out would be in a freshwater spot like the Zancudo River, Vinewood Hills, or the Land Act Reservoir. Details on which fish you can catch can be seen by selecting the individual fishing zone icons on the map.

Once you get to a Fishing Zone, stop in place and get out of your boat, hit the ( , ) key (if it fails, you can use the period key too) and this will anchor your boat in place. Now press E, you’ll get this quick-time event on your screen:

Press E when the red bar becomes green; it’ll repeatedly shrink and expand so if you miss it, you get more chances to catch it in time. The yellow bar is how long you have until time expires and the fish is off the hook.

Once you’ve caught enough fish, navigate to your inventory and gut them all.

M → Inventory  → Fish → Gut all fish

Or

M → Inventory  → Gut Knife → Gut fish

Once your inventory is full, and you've gutted the fish, put your fish meat in your boat's trunk. This can be done in two ways:

B → Trunk → Put → Left Arrow on fish meat (or use Put All option if no other weighted items are in your inventory)

Or

M → Car/Vehicle Options → Access Trunk → Put → Left Arrow on fish meat (or use Put All option if no other weighted items are in your inventory)

Assuming your inventory and your trunk are now full of fish meat, navigate to a Fish Importer and start selling the meat. In exchange for the meat, you will receive Money and Fishing XP, alternatively, you can sell the fish meat to other players at the Marketplace in exchange for more money, but no experience. If you do decide to sell to players it might be useful to store the fish meat in any Self Storage. Make sure to use Storage Cards to avoid massive fees.

The fish meat can also be used in the Trucking (Refrigerated) route.

The type/quality of fish you receive depends on location, a good rule of thumb is the harder to get to, the better the fish you receive will be. The Oil Rig is the best location for traditional fishing. It is also worth noting that fishing in rainy or stormy weather will yield the greatest amount of fish as the catch time is halved during these periods.

Gut Knives

Gutting with a knife can be bound to a key. For more info, check out the Binds page.

A Gut Knife (15kg) can be purchased at the Market(Fishing/Diving) for $13,500,000 ($13.5M).
The Gut Knife can gut all the fish in your inventory at once rather than manually gut each fish. (You still need the required level to gut the fish!)
When gutting fish, there is a roughly 1:100 chance of the knife upgrading to a StatTrakTM variant.

A StatTrakTM Gut Knife will track how many fish you gut, which can be seen in the item description.
When gutting fish with the StatTrakTM Gut Knife, there is yet another 1:100 chance of the knife upgrading to a new form. When you upgrade to 1 (one) of the 4 (four) variants of the StatTrak gut knife, it cannot upgrade to a new variant ever again.

The different forms are:

  • StatTrakTM Gut Knife | Autotronic, which allows you to gut any fish regardless of level. It also has a doubled gut speed.
  • StatTrakTM Gut Knife | Tiger Tooth, which is twice as fast as the Autotronic and only weighs 10kg (from 15kg).
  • StatTrakTM Gut Knife | Weightless, which is five times as fast as the Tiger Tooth and as the name implies weighs nothing.
  • StatTrakTM Gut Knife | Grinder, which automatically guts fish as you obtain them.

Each new form is rarer to get than the previous one, making the autotronic a very common result.

The StatTrak™ Gut Knife can also be bought for 50 Skill Level Tokens, skipping the Gut Knife entirely.

Net Fishing

Once you reach Fishing Level: 30, you can use a Reefer to do trawling. On the map, you will find green versions of the fish anchor icons . They become visible if you are in a compatible trawler boat. There’s a smaller amount of them, but once you get there you will see there is a large radius. As you navigate through the marked area with your boat, you will periodically get small bundles of fish - there will also be various checkpoints that appear within the circle (they appear randomly as long as you are within the radius), navigate through them to get additional bonus fish. After reaching Fishing Level: 70 you can start using Tug for net fishing. Everything that applies to normal fishing zones for converting and selling fish meat applies here too.

DEVELOPERS NOTE: During "Rainy" weather, the chance of receiving extra bonus fish increases.
During "Stormy" weather, this chance is increased again.
Amount = Random(-3 + BonusFish, 3 + BonusFish), where BonusFish = 0 by default, 1 during Rain, 2 during Storm.

Pot Fishing

  1. To start, head to Market(Fishing/Diving) and buy pots for skill tokens (1 pot costs 1 skill token).
    • You can buy crab pots, lobster pots and sand pots. Crab pots give more fish, but less immediate experience. Lobster pots give more immediate experience, but less fish. Sand pots will give out sand instead of fish
      • In other words, crab pots give more overall experience when the fish meat is sold at the Fish Importer, but are also the best option for players looking for more fish meat.
  2. Place them on any open water (pools won't work) and wait for your meat and EXP while doing something completely different.
Keep in mind that you can only place 40 pots at the same time - there is one shared limit for all pot types.

Depending on your level, pots can stay in the water for a different amount of time - 3 hours at Fishing level 1 and up to 22 hours at Fishing level 100. After peak time you have about 24 hours to pick up your loot. After this period, pots will start to degrade one step every 12 hours.

Yield can degrade up to 50% of the max value if not collected on time.

You get 6 crabs for every hour the pot is in the water until you reach the maximum limit. The maximum timeframe is displayed, when you lay them on the ocean's floor.
You get 50x Fish Meat for gutting of 1 crab.
