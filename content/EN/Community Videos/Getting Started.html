Basic knowledge

Below you will find the answers to some of the most common questions.
Many other questions are answered on the FAQ and Commands pages.

Question

Method

Alternative Method

Inventory

Press "M" > Inventory

Access Wiki In-game

Press "F7"

Press "T" > Type for example /f7

Autopilot / Tempomat

Press "Y" (autopilot on planes only works above 700ft)

Hide the chat

Press "T" > Press "F1" - Close the chat and wait

Press "CTRL"+"T" > Click Hide Chat - Close the chat and wait

Change to driver seat

Press "T" and type /seat -1

Press "M" > Player Options > Change Seats

Seatbelt

Press "M" > Player Options > Toggle Options > Permanent Seatbelt

Sell cars

Press "M" > Player Options > Interface Options > Toggle Legacy Garage
Method to sell for 15% of the price at no additional cost

Shipping Container
Method to sell to players at the cost of making the container

Change minimap size and zoom

Hold "Z"

Change MPH to KM/H

Press "M" > Player Options > Interface Options > Change Mph to Km/h
