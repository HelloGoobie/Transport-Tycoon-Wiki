Erasers can only be placed in the Speedo Express or Mk1, Mk2, Mk3, or Mk4 Trailers. All other trailers will show an error when adding to trunk.

Erasers is an item that can be picked up at the Cargo Seller: Erasers and can be exported at the Cargo Buyer: Erasers. It is part of the Trucking job.
