Usage

The Shipping Container can be used at the Vehicle Shipping marker on the Elysian Island to turn owned vehicles into an item that can be sold on the Marketplace. Only vehicles in the player's garage can be put in shipping containers, excluding trailers under the MK10, as the vehicle needs to be entered in order to be sold.
