As an Aerial Firefighter, you will be navigating the skies responding to and putting out large fires all over the map to get rewarded with Money, Firefighter & Airline EXP as well with EXP Tokens.

Getting Started

1. Go to the Specialized Job marker Specialized Job(12/-) at the LSIA Fire Department / Crash Team Building. (Shown in the following picture)

2. Go to the orange Job Selector circle and choose Aerial Firefighter.

3. To buy your water bomber, you will need to visit any Aircraft Dealership Aircraft Shop.

4. Go to any Aircraft Garage Aircraft Garage and spawn your water bomber.

5. Remain on standby until an assignment is given to you.

6. You will receive an assignment with a region attached. Fly to the area, on the map you can find the fire marked with Fire and an orange circle on the ground.

7. To put out the fire, open the water bays using “Space” and fly over the fires. Once you put out all the fires you will receive payment.

8. Wait for another assignment and repeat!

You’ll likely be out of water very quickly, you can either land on the water with the water bay open if your water bomber is capable to refill, or you can land at any airport and park on the Re-fill Zone Refill Zone
