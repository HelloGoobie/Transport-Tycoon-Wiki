Beach Cleaner

Job

Beach Cleaner

Location

Vespucci Beach

Beach Clean Up is one of the Specialized Jobs that is available within Transport Tycoon . As a beach cleaner you will clean the beaches of Vespucci Beach, gathering sand and trash in the meantime.

The sand can be used for Trucking, the trash can be saved up to make Treasure Chests at the Recycling Plant in the Grand Senora Desert.

Getting Started

  • Go to the Specialized Job marker  near the Del Perro Pier at the lifeguard station.
  • Go to the orange job selector circle and choose Beach Clean Up.
  • Go to the blue circle and Go on Duty.
  • You should see 5 question marks on your map.
    • This is where you spawn your beach clean up vehicle. Spawn the appropriate vehicle for your Garbage level.
  • You should see three sand excavation zones along the beach. Once you enter these very large blue circles, you will start to gain Garbage Collector experience, sand, and trash as you drive your beach clean up vehicle in the circle.
  •  Occasionally you will see red circles inside the big blue area. If you drive through them, you will receive a bonus!

Tips

  • Since the nearby job center marker also includes the Trucking job, you can bring a trailer and place it nearby to be able to store your sand for transporting in bulk.
    • This job is a good alternative for gathering sand if you're not fond of the Quarry Rubble Route.
  • It is also a good method of leveling up your garbage skill if you’re not a fan of the Garbage routes, a bonus of doing it this way is that you will accumulate Sand which can be sold to other players or used to make concrete or other goods.
