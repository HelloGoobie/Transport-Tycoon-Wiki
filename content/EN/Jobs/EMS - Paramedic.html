--- title: EMS / Paramedic categories: - Jobs ---


The paramedics of Transport Tycoon are lifesavers. Performing this job will be saving the lives of many poor AI.
There is one main objective as EMS: Receive AI calls to revive and transport them to the hospital.

Getting started

You must have level 5 strength to do EMS missions!
  • Visit any Job Center (except for Life Invader) and select the EMS job.
  • Purchase an EMS vehicle from the Car Dealership  or a helicopter from the Aircraft Dealership .
    • The helicopters require at least level 5 Helicopter Pilot.
  • Optionally, you can get a paramedic uniform from Pillbox Hospital .
  • Players looking to earn slightly more experience should consider carrying defib kits (+10% exp).

Objective: Receive AI calls to revive and transport them to the hospital

  • Head to one of the 10 Paramedic Stations. EMS Helicopter can land on the rooftop (If the hospital has a designated Heli landing Pad).
  • Walk to the entrance or rooftop main door (For EMS Helicopter) and press E to go on duty.
  • Wait for an automatic call to occur.
  • Follow the GPS to the location marked on your map.
  • Press E to revive the AI.
  • The revived patient will get into your EMS Vehicle.
  • Head back to the hospital now marked on your map with the patient. To drop off the patient you must drive your ambulance inside the orange circle or there will be an orange circle on the rooftop to land with the EMS Helicopter.
  • Congratulations, you’ve successfully completed an AI callout and will now receive Medic Exp!
In the event where you're unable to revive a patient, the patient is not reachable or the patient is stuck after reviving: Enter your vehicle and attempt to drive away. If the patient does not teleport inside the vehicle, cancel the mission by pressing M ► Stop Job and then head back to the Paramedic Station to go back on duty.
If when reviving an patient while on duty the patient refuses to enter the helicopter then fly to the dropoff point, get out and get in a couple of times, the subject should eventually teleport and you'll finish the job.

Streak

Be sure to do at least 1 callout in a 2 day period to keep your streak, otherwise it will reset back to 0

As you deliver patients to the hospital, you build up a streak. At every streak threshold you earn more Experience and EXP Tokens for every patient dropped off.

This threshold occurs at specific intervals. starting from 10 and increasing progressively. Initially, it rises by 10 until reaching 50. From there, the jumps become larger, first reaching 100, then 250, and continuing to grow. The increments move from 500 to 750, then 1000, and continue in steps of 250 until reaching 2500. 

(20 30 40 50 100 250 500 750 1000 1250 1500 1750 2000 2250 2500)

Your streak will reset after 48 real life hours.

Your streak is visible under M→Identity. Otherwise it is also visible to the right of your minimap when on duty and available, but not while On Route.
