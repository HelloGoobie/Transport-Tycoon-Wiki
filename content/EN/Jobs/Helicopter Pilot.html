Getting Started

Getting started with Helicopter Pilot is pretty straightforward. To get your dream job taking customers sight-seeing across Los Santos and Blaine County you should:

  • Have $60,000 in Cash
  • Become a Heli-Pilot at any Job Center (except for Life Invader)
  • Purchase the first Heli from Aircraft Dealership

If you have done all the above, simply take your heli and go to any of the Job Start locations.

Start Locations:

  • LSIA - Near the main terminal
  • Higgins Heli-Tours - Near the Boat Shop
  • SSIA - On the airport helipad

Once you have started the job you will be given a location on the map to collect passengers from. After the passengers have boarded you can start your route. From these routes, you will be rewarded with money and Skill Tokens. As you rank up, you will unlock more helicopters to purchase. The higher tiered helicopters have longer route lengths with more stops but in return yield more money and Skill Tokens.
