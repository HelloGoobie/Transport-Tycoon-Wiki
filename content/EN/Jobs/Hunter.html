The Wildlife Hunter job is usually regarded as a good and entertaining way to make money when you start. However, it is wise to follow the Getting Started Guide first to increase strength and inventory space. If you like sniper rifles and sniping pixelated animals, this job is for you. Watch out for dangerous kitties and deer-looking bears!

Getting Started with Hunting

  1. Head to any Job Center (except for Life Invader) and select the Wildlife Hunter job.
  2. Buy a Hunting Vehicle from the Car Dealership .
    1. Optionally: Buy hunting Permits and Formulas from the Market(Fishing/Diving) .
  3. Drive to the Hunting Office (Hunting level 1 office is located South-West from Paleto Bay).
  4. At the Hunting Office enter the orange circle and press E to receive hunting missions and equipment.
  5. Go to each mission zone shown on the map and hunt down "expected number" of animals visible on the map blip in the center of each zone.
    1. Skin hunted animals (they will be marked on the map) by pressing E when close to the animal.
    2. After clearing out all mission zones you can accept more at the Hunting Office once again.
  6. Drive to the Raven Slaughterhouse to process your animal materials into Meat.
  7. Sell your Meat at the Meat Buyer in Paleto Bay. You will receive money ($250 per) and Player experience.
Animals are client-sided so no one can steal your prey.
Keep in mind that your ammunition in sniper rifle is limited so try not to miss too many times. At level 50 you will unlock a secondary weapon.
Avoid using the wardrobe while on a mission. Using the wardrobe will remove all your weapons.
Hunting on Cayo Perico and can't find the animals? Look up in the trees! CP is a little buggy, so sometimes they spawn where they shouldn't.

Hunting Experience can be gained by:

  • Hunting down mission animals
    • Optionally hunting down non-mission animals, but for less EXP
  • Finishing whole mission zone
  • Skinning mission animals
  • Processing animal materials into meat

Hunting Items

Before starting a hunting mission you can buy additional items from Market(Fishing/Diving) to increase the efficiency of the hunt.
The items below are consumed automatically when you start a hunting mission at one of the Hunting Offices.

Item name Cost It reads
Hunting Permit 2 skill level tokens A document allowing you to hunt in more locations per hunting mission.
Hunters Formula 6 skill level tokens A highly synthetic product that wild animals love, it's a perfect way to lure them out of hiding!

Hunting Offices

Along with your Hunting level, you will get access to more hunting offices to use - they will unlock more profitable types of animals, but hunting zones might also be further away from each other.

  • Park Ranger's Office - Hunting level 1
  • Hunter's Lodge - Hunting level 25
  • Cayo Perico Pest Patrol - Hunting level 25
  • Stab City Hunters - Hunting level 50
  • Grapeseed Poachers - Hunting level 75
Hunting vehicles have a dynamic trunk size starting at 30 kg and going to 2000 kg at level 100 hunting.
