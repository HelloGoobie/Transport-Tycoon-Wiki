--- title: IAA Delivery categories: - Jobs - Specialized Jobs ---

IAA Delivery is a specialized job in which you need to bring the IAA Coffee & Documents to different locations inside the facility to get player & <a data-p

"strength">strength exp as well as your money.

Getting Started

1. Go to the Specialized Job marker Specialized Job at the satellite dishes near Sandy Shores.

2. Go to the yellow floating marker and enter the IAA Facility.

3. Once inside, go to the orange job selector and select IAA Delivery.

4. Go to the green circle beside the selector and purchase IAA Coffee and IAA Documents. These are what you will be delivering to different desks in the facility, if you plan on doing this job for a while you should purchase a lot. You can also come back to this location and purchase more at any time.

5. Wait for a call out to appear in the top right area of your screen. The callout details the supplies you will need, as seen in the image below.

6. Go to the location marked on your map as Job (IAA delivery) and delivery the coffee and documents. All locations are inside this facility.

7. You will keep receiving callouts until you switch jobs.

To get rid of the callout after already switching the job, simply do M > Stop job.
