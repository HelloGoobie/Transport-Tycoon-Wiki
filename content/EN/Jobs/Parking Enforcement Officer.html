--- title: Parking Enforcement Officer categories: - Jobs - Specialized Jobs ---

As a Parking Enforcement Officer, you will protect the streets and citizens of Los Santos from unruly parking and dish out parking fines!

Getting Started

1. Go to the Specialized Job marker Specialized Job(9/-) at the S.A Parking Council HQ (Shown in the following picture).

2. In the Specialized Job marker, select the Parking Enforcement Officer job

3. There will be a white circle where you can spawn the PEO vehicle on the right side of the entrance in a parking space.

4. Once you've spawned the PEO vehicle slowly drive next to parked cars to scan them. After scanning a parked vehicle is a chance to ticket the vehicle and receive a ticket commission.

5. Find the next vehicle and repeat!

Demonstration

Unlockable Vehicles

Brute Enforcer- 500 scan in one session
LCPD Premier- Issue 100 parking tickets
LSPD Aleutian- Issue 250 Parking tickets
Prison Coach- Finish at least 100 Bus routes.

These Vehicles can be acquired upon completing the above challenges at Davis County Sherriff office.
