Getting Started

  1. Go to one of the Market (Pizza)Pizza locations in order to get the Pizza Delivery job.
    • Pizza Delivery requires your Strength to be level 30.
  2. Then go to your nearest vehicle purchasing location Car Dealer, either at Simons dealership in the city or a faction dealership.
  3. Purchase one of the FREE Food Delivery Vehicles under Vehicles → Events.
  4. Once you actually get to start the job, a menu will appear in the top right corner of your screen. This tells you the food and location of what the "customer" has ordered.
  5. Go to the Pizza MarketPizza again and pick up the items needed for the order, for example, Pizza/Expensive Water/Fries/Chicken Nuggets, etc.
    • Alternatively, take your time to stock up and completely fill the trunk.
  6. Follow your map waypointDelivery to your customer's address, and then simply walk over the yellow circle to deliver the items.
  7. You get given money, strength, and player exp for your delivery efforts.
  8. Simply repeat and increase your position on the leaderboard.

You have to use the event vehicles when doing this job. Keep in mind that the pizza delivery vehicles are a very useful because of the trunk capacity.
