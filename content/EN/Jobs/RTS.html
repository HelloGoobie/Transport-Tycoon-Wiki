Rockwell Transport Solutions
R.T.S.
The company has closed the job is now public for all players

R.T.S. (also known as Rockwell Transport Solutions) is a former company, with its jobs now available to everyone. You are responsible to either deliver ground vehicles or take to the skies and deliver helicopters and planes . Successful perfect deliveries of regular ground vehicles will award you with that vehicles' one-time-use spawncard.

Getting Started

To start any aspect of R.T.S. , you need to visit the headquarters and change your job at the job marker.

This is located at the Arcadius Business Center. The job marker is located left of the entrance (exterior). You can choose between R.T.S. Aviator, R.T.S. Professional and R.T.S. Transporter.

R.T.S. Transporter

As an R.T.S. Transporter, you can deliver regular vehicles as well as heavier vehicles . Regular vehicles will be faster and will also award you with a one-time-use spawncard upon successful delivery while heavier vehicles awards are doubled per individual delivery due to more time spend. Both options will award you with some cash as well as Racing and Business Experience. The more you complete deliveries in a session, the higher the rewards.

Go to a Vehicle Pickup or Heavy Pickup icon, they can be found all over the map.
A menu will show up, giving you a selection of 2 to 25 vehicles (see #Delivery Milestones). If you chose for heavy you will not be given a selection. Once you have chosen the vehicle you wish to deliver, a destination will be marked on your map. Drive to the destination however you wish, but make sure to not damage the vehicle too much. You will not be able to repair the vehicle and risk losing the spawn card reward.

R.T.S. Aviator

Failure to properly use ATC can get you punished, so make sure you understand how it works!

As an R.T.S. Aviator, you will be delivering small to medium-sized planes and helicopters. Go to an Aviator Pickup icon. A destination will be marked on your map. Fly to the destination, making sure you correctly use ATC. Upon delivery you will be awarded with small amounts of Business, Airline Pilot and Racing Experience.

R.T.S. Professional

As an R.T.S. Professional, you will be able to do both Transporter and Aviator job deliveries.

Delivery Milestones

Reaching certain total delivery milestones will allow you to choose from more vehicles at a time. This begins at a selection of 2 and goes up to 25 at 2000 deliveries. Your total R.T.S. deliveries can be viewed with the in-game /stats command. Alternatively you can also view this in Discord by visiting the #bot-commands channel and typing @Tycoon Stats Bot stats .
