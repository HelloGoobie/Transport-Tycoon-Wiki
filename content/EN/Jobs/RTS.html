Rockwell Transport Solutions


R.T.S.


R.T.S. RTS 22px (also known as Rockwell Transport Solutions) is a former company, with its jobs now available to everyone. You are responsible to either deliver ground vehicles or take to the skies and deliver helicopters and planes RTSAviatorPickup. Successful perfect deliveries of regular ground vehicles RTSVehiclePickup 22px will award you with that vehicles' one-time-use spawncard.

Getting Started

To start any aspect of R.T.S. , you need to visit the headquarters RTSHQ 22px and change your job at the job marker.-108.2 -631.9 255,125,0 RTSHQ.png true This is located at the Arcadius Business Center. The job marker is located left of the entrance (exterior). You can choose between R.T.S. Aviator, R.T.S. Professional and R.T.S. Transporter.

R.T.S. Transporter

As an R.T.S. Transporter, you can deliver regular vehicles RTSVehiclePickup 22px as well as heavier vehicles RTSHeavyPickup 22px. Regular vehicles will be faster and will also award you with a one-time-use spawncard upon successful delivery while heavier vehicles awards are doubled per individual delivery due to more time spend. Both options will award you with some cash as well as Racing and Business Experience. The more deliveries you do, the higher the rewards (see Streak).

Go to a Vehicle Pickup RTSVehiclePickup 22px or Heavy Pickup RTSHeavyPickup 22px icon, they can be found all over the map.
A menu will show up, giving you a selection of 2 to 25 vehicles (see Delivery Milestones). If you chose for heavy you will not be given a selection. Once you have chosen the vehicle you wish to deliver, a destination will be marked on your map. Drive to the destination however you wish, but make sure to not damage the vehicle too much. You will not be able to repair the vehicle and risk losing the spawn card reward.

R.T.S. Aviator

As an R.T.S. Aviator, you will be delivering small to medium-sized planes and helicopters. Go to an Aviator Pickup RTSAviatorPickup icon. A destination will be marked on your map. Fly to the destination. Upon delivery you will be awarded with small amounts of Business, Airline Pilot and Racing Experience.

R.T.S. Professional

As an R.T.S. Professional, you will be able to do both Transporter and Aviator job deliveries.

Delivery Milestones

Reaching certain total delivery milestones will allow you to choose from more vehicles at a time. This begins at a selection of 2 and goes up to 25 at 2000 deliveries. Your total R.T.S. deliveries can be viewed with the in-game /stats command. Alternatively you can also view this in Discord by visiting the #bot-commands channel and typing @Tycoon Stats Bot stats .

  • 3 at 10
  • 4 at 50
  • 5 at 100
  • 6 at 200
  • 7 at 300
  • 8 at 400
  • 9 at 500
  • 12 at 750
  • 15 at 1000
  • 20 at 1500
  • 25 at 2000

Streak

The streak increases rewards linearly and starts at 7.5 EXP and 30 000$. The RTS streak has currently no maximum.

The increase is +0.075 EXP and 300$ per delivery
