--- title: Boat Shop categories: - Locations ---

This is the Boat Shop. Here you can find all vehicles with their prices and designated fuel type. After purchasing your watercraft, you can spawn it at a Watercraft Garage Boat Garage 27.
Boat Shops are marked with Watercraft Shop on the map. They can be found in La Puerta (somewhat north of Los Santos International Airport) and at Galilee (on the northern side of the Alamo Sea).
