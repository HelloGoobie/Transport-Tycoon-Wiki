L.S.I.A Upgrade Station

Type

Workshop

Location

L.S.I.A

The L.S.I.A Upgrade Station is a location used for upgrading vehicles. It is 1 of the 3 upgrade station locations that are available in the game. The other 2 stations are: Alien Upgrade Station and Sports Upgrade Station

The location icons will only appear on the map when you're in an upgradable vehicle
