Businesses Business Saleare places for you to invest your wealth to get a return in profits over a period of time.

Bonuses

Businesses Business generate a bonus that can be picked up every 3 hours until their maximum capacity at 16 stacks. This takes 48 hours of real-life time in total. It is recommended to claim before reaching 16 stacks, as once it reaches 16 stacks it will stop generating extra money. If you buy an "Extra Business Day" through the Tebex Shop, you can receive another 24 hour period to obtain Business Cash Stacks, making your new maximum 24 stacks. This can be bought multiple times to stack more and more possible vacant days before it caps out.

You can find the bonuses on the map indicated by a yellow Dollar sign Business Bonus, indicating the business has at least one bonus ready for collecting, or a blue Dollar sign Business Full, indicating that the business has reached maxed stacks and will not generate any more bonuses. Every bonus has a chance to give a little bit of extra cash stacks on pickup. Experience from Business bonuses does not stack, if you wish to optimize leveling, you should go collect the bonus every 3 hours. This can become tedious when many businesses are owned, which is why using a Business Bonus Collector will be useful.

Business Cash Stacks

Collecting the bonuses from your Businesses gives an item called "Business Cash Stacks". These items are worth 1000$ each and will be redeemed upon gaining experience in any job other than business. They are redeemed in the ratio of 50K Dollars per 1 XP at level 1 business, and 150k dollars per 1 XP at level 100 business. The ratio also changes throughout the different business levels.

Business Bonus Collector

The Train Yards and Ship Yard Business Bonus cannot be collected manually. They can only be collected via a Bonus CollectorBusiness Bonus Collector. This requires either the purchase of the Pacific Standard Bank or requires joining a faction that has acquired the Collector Perk. Players that own the Pacific Standard Bank will be taxed at 20% for using the collector. Players without the bank will be taxed at 30%.

Getting Started

To start investing into businesses, head to Wenger Institute next to the City Hall to buy your first business for $15k.

To unlock different businesses, you will need to level up your Businesses skill level - it can be done via collecting business cash stacks or by doing the RTS or CollinsCo Cabbies job. Business level requirement for specific businesses can be viewed by hovering and clicking over the business that is shown on your map.
Keep in mind that EXP doesn't stack at the collector, so try to collect it as often as you can.

Train yards have linked storages - You can access that storage from any of the other Trainyards that you own.

Earning Business Experience

Besides claiming your business bonuses, you can also earn small amounts of experience in the business skill from the following activities:

  • Delivering vehicles via RTS
  • Transporting passengers as a CollinsCo Cabbie
