The City Hall

Type

Spawn Point
(Passive Zone)

Location

Rockford Hills, Los Santos

The City Hall is the hub area all new players spawn at. It is also a Passive Zone.

The City Hall offers access to:

  • The Premium / VIP Market
  • The Identity Creation Menu (Currently serves no actual purpose).
  • Free bicycle spawner
  • Navigation assistant
