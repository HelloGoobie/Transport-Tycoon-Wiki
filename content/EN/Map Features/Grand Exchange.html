The Grand Exchange Grand Exchange is a centralized voucher turn-in location for ease-of-access for players. In exchange for any vouchers obtained before the change, players will receive Bonus XP items relative to the voucher type at the Grand Exchange.

Grand Exchange Location on Map:

Just north of City Hall

Vouchers can be exchanged in multiples of 10 or 1000 Vouchers will now automatically turn into Tokens when giving them to other players or receiving them from Storages or other players.
