--- title: Semi Spawners categories: - Map Features - Trucking ---

When you own a trailer but don't have or want to buy a semi truck, you can spawn one free of charge at certain locations throughout the map.

Current known locations

  • Los Santos Airport
  • Los Santos Docks
  • Downtown Los Santos
  • Paleto Bay Gas Station
  • Sandy Shores Garage

When you are near one of these locations, a white question mark should appear on your mini-map. This is where you can spawn the semi's
