--- title: Vehicle Shop categories: - Map Features ---

Vehicles can be bought at a Car Dealer vehicle shop, which then become available for spawning at a garage. Vehicles require fuel to run, and the top speed of a vehicle may be different from vanilla cars. They can be found in Central LS (Simeon's car dealership), Sandy Shores, Paleto Bay, Cayo Perico and possibly at your faction. Owned vehicles can be sold to other players using a Shipping Container.

The availability of vehicles to purchase rotates each day. Once a vehicle becomes available it can be bought from the store for a few days. This means that if you are looking for a specific vehicle and it is not visible in the vehicle shop, you may need to wait a few days for it to become available. Currently company vehicles are not able to be purchased. Purchase cars wisely, because you need to spend around $154,500,000,000 if you want to buy all vehicles.
