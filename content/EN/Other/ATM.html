--- title: ATM ---


An ATM allows you to withdraw and deposit cash with the Maze Bank. ATM's can be found all over the map.

There are several ways to reach an ATM:

  • At Public ATM locations
  • At purchasable businesses locations
  • A Portable ATM Phone Extension (Requires Premium)
  • At a Faction ATM

Purpose of ATM's and Banks

There is currently no reason to store your cash in a bank, but who walks around the street with billions in pocket change?

Previously, the ATM's were used to create gift cards. They were used to avoid tax, as giving cash money to people used to be taxed. As of the Changelogs of 20 February 2021, This is no longer the case, and gift cards can not be made anymore.
