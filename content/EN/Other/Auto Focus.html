--- title: Auto Focus ---

Auto Focus (or Auto-Focus) refers to a chat function that allows the player to make the chat disappear, not reappearing for every message that is received.

To enable Auto Focus: press CTRL+T, click on Auto-Focus to hide the chat (it should turn <span style="color: #ff0000;>red), then close the chat and wait. The chat will fade away in a few seconds.
The button is a toggle, so pressing <span style="color: #ff0000;>Auto-Focus again makes the chat pop up as usual.
