Blessings

Blessings (also known as Chat Prefix Icons) are emotes that can be placed in front of your name in the in-game chat. They are obtained from opening Blessing Packs, which can be purchased in game at the Market(Premium/VIP).

Using and setting a blessing

You can open a Blessing Pack in your inventory by selecting the pack and hitting "Use." A random Blessing from the series will then appear in your inventory, which you can then either sell to other players or Use to put its icon into your Chat Prefix Icon list. Opening more than one blessing pack will require you to purchase and use a Cursed Dice from the Market(Premium/VIP) first.

To open the Chat Prefix Icon list and set the prefix icon you obtained from a blessing, navigate to
M -> Player Options -> Change Chat Appearance -> Change Chat Prefix Icon.
