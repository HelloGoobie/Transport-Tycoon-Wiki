Restrictions

If your Strength is above level 30, ensure you still set up your Strength level as 30. Any level above 30 Strength does not give extra inventory space but the calculator will still add extra space that you do not have. If you wish to not use your inventory at all during your trucking, set your strength as 0 in the calculator. This way it will not consider your inventory for the trip calculations.

Due to the in-game browser not storing any information, you will have to refill in your alternative recipes, API key and UserID in the settings every time if you wish to use those features with the in-game browser.

The in-game browsers' size is rather small, in some instances you'll have to scroll to the left of right. To more easily do this, hold LeftShift while moving the scroll wheel up or down to move left or right respectively.
