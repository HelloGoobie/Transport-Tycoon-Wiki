--- title: Challenges ---

Challenge tokens do not currently reward anything so far (last checked on 27 September 2026)

warn

Every player receives a set of challenges to complete.

Challenges can be viewed by navigating through M►Identity►Challenges, or by typing /challenges in chat.
A temporary bar at the bottom right will show when you make progress towards your challenge(s). You can use the command /toggle_xp to keep the progress bar on screen for the remainder of your session.

Daily Challenges

Completing all Daily Challenges will reward you with a Challenge Token (Daily).

The following list is non-exhaustive

  • Finish 2 missions as a Hunter
  • Bury 2 bones (Prospecting)
  • Complete 2 callouts as a Firefighter

Weekly Challenges

For each Weekly Challenge completed you will receive a prize.

The following list is non-exhaustive

  • Mine 50 rocks in the quarry as a Miner
  • Transport 30 aircraft as an RTS Aviator
  • Complete 30 activities on Cayo Perico
  • Complete 30 routes as a Bus Driver
  • Craft 40 houses as a Trucker
