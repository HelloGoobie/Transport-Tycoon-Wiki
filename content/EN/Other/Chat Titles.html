Chat Titles

Chat titles are an addition next to the player's name, that allows them to stand out in the in-game chat.
At the same time, they can show what they own or have accomplished.


There are different types of chat titles, such as:

  • 1 Million+ Experience Titles.
    • These titles are the most difficult to obtain- usually requiring the most time & knowledge about the game from a player.
  • Event Titles.
    • Event titles are typically achievable only during special occasional/seasonal events which happen on the Tycoon servers.
    • You can obtain these titles at the end of the event, more information is usually shared during such event.
    • Some of them may be tradable with players.
  • Server / Captain's Hour - Supporter Titles.
    • You can support the Tycoon Developers to keep the server up or support the Captain's Hour Host by buying different goods from the Tebex /shop.
    • Additionally, you can check out the #info channel on the Tycoon Gaming Discord for the Patreon link or press here, where you can find more supporter roles.
  • Total Days Logged In Titles.
    • These titles are awarded for reaching specific total logged-in days.
  • Special / Unique / Other Titles.
    • These kinds of titles are difficult to obtain, unobtainable, or just very rare.
    • Verified roles are usually given by the Devs to the members of the community who are advertising/creating Transport Tycoon related content.
    • This can be done by sharing content on different social media platforms or by streaming gameplay on Twitch, YouTube, etc.
    • In this table, you can also find chat titles that don't fit into other categories.
  • Company Titles.
    • When Companies still existed, you were able to use their titles, starting with only one.
    • After some time, when you ranked up / rose in the company hierarchy, you would have the option to use other titles from the respective company.
To switch chat titles, go to M → Player Options → Change Chat Appearance → Change Chat Title.
