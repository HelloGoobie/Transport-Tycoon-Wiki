Santa will occationally Request certain items at Legion Square, in return for delivering them Santa will give you a present which you can open.

Golden Gun Bakery

The Golden Gun Bakery is Located in Paleto Bay and is used to bake cookies. To Bake 13 Cookies you will need 5 Sugar, 1 Flour and 1 Egg.

  • You can obtain Sugar by Farming and planting Sugar Cane Seeds which are obtained through chests or the Famers Market
  • You can obtain Flour by Farming and planting Wheat Seeds which are obtained through chests or other Players
  • You can obtain 'Eggs by Farming and collecting them from the chickens (100 Per Day During Event)

Snow Plowing

To Start Snow Plowing head to one of the 3 spawn locations.

  • Location 1 - Paleto Bay
  • Location 2 - Grand Senora Desert (Near Filtering Plant)
  • Location 3 - Autopia Pkwy (Near Airport)
  • Once you have obtained a Snow Plow head on the road and ensure you have began salting the roads to Earn Money and Candy Canes.
  • When snowplowing you will slowly lose salt that you can refill at locations around the map to continue earning rewards.

Caroling

Between the hours of 8 PM and 6 AM (in-game) you can go to doors and press E to Carol at houses. Each door will give candy canes on a successful completion of the Quick Time Event. The best area is Grove Street and Appartments nearby that area. Mirror Park is also a good area.

Doors can only be Caroled once per night, no need to relog to go Caroling again just wait for 8pm again!

Courier

This can be done by delivering presents around los santos and receiving Candy Canes for each delivery More information can be found about the Courier Job in Courier
