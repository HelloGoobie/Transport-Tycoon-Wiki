--- title: Daily ---

Daily may refer to:

  • Treasure Chest [Daily], a Treasure chest that used to be given every day as a login reward.
  • Skill of the Day, a base experience boost ranging from +25% to +50%, applied to one skill. A new skill is chosen at random every day at midnight UTC time.
    • To see the current skill of the day, Look in M → Identity, or look for the SOTD in the top of the channel list in the Discord.
