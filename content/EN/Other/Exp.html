--- title: Exp ---

Exp may refer to:

  • Experience, points earned for completing job tasks.
  • EXP Tokens, tokens that can be turned into Skill Bonus EXP.
  • Hidden EXP Boosts, white circles across the map that can give a variety of XP boosts.
  • Double EXP Ticket, a purchasable server-wide 100% base Experience boost. Can be bought in the Tebex Shop for real-life currency, or via the Marketplace with in-game wealth.
