--- title: Forklift Operator categories: - Jobs - Specialized Jobs ---

Forklift Operator is a job in which you use a forklift to pick up pallets, place then on a trailer to earn money.

Getting Started

  • Start by making your way to the far South East building at the Docks.

  • Head inside the building to the Vehicle Warehouse Icon, where you will be able to spawn a forklift.
  • Once in the Forklift you should see a green holographic cylinder around a pallet.
  • Once identifying the correct pallet drive towards it and it will the holographic cylinder will turn white.
  • Then pick up the indicated pallet and you should see a truck and trailer outside one of the loading bay doors.
  • Drive to the Trailer with pallet and place it on the trailer
  • Repeat until the trailer is full

Once full the truck will drive away and you will receive payment and repeat the process.
