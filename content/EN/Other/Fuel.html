--- title: Fuel ---

Many modes of transportation such as vehicles, planes, helicopters and boats require fuel to operate. The type of fuel required may vary, for example whether you are driving a car or a truck. Electric vehicles and trains do not require refuelling. You can also purchase an upgrade to disable fuel cost or to disable the fuel system completely for you through the Tebex Shop and support the development of the server.

Fuel Types

There are different types of fuels, that can be crafted with Trucking at the Oil Depository or obtained at petrol stations, ports and airports:

  • Boat Fuel
  • Diesel Fuel
  • Helicopter Fuel
  • Petrol Fuel
  • Plane Fuel
