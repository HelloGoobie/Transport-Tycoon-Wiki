The Golden Bun Bakery is a seasonal location, available in the Christmas Holidays, in Paleto Bay where you can bake Christmas Cookies using resources acquired from farming.

Getting Started

To get started, head to any Job Center Job Center job center (except for LifeInvader) and change your job to Farmer. This will allow you to access the required activities involved in Farming part of making Christmas Cookies.

  • You will require Chickens (for eggs), Wheat seeds (for the flour) and Sugar Cane seeds (for the sugar). (If you are stuck on how to obtain these seeds check out the farming obtaining seeds wiki page.)
  • All info needed for the Farming aspects such as planting, cultivating and harvesting your Wheat and Sugar Cane, and buying Chickens, can be found on the Farming wiki page.

Once you have gathered all the required resources, head over to the Golden Bun Bakery, found in Paleto Bay (Map location can be found here) to convert your Farming: Wheat and Farming: Sugar Cane into their respective Flour and Sugar, required in baking Christmas Cookies.
