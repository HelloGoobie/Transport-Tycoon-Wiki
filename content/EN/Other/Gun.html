--- title: Gun ---

Reconnecting, changing outfit or starting the <a data-p

"Garbage Collector">Garbage Collector job will remove all your weapons.
stopping the Hunter job ( M►Stop Job) will remove all hunting weapons.

warn

Guns are only obtained through and used for a few activities, listed below:

  • The Hunter job gives guns to be used to hunt animals. As your Hunter level increases, you'll be given newer and better upgraded weapons.
  • Firearms can sometimes be found through Prospecting, which can be cleaned to reveal a random weapon.
  • Across the map there are several hidden easter egg gun pickup spots. Due to them being easter eggs, it will be up to you to find them.
  • In the Legacy server, the P.I.G.S. company needs guns for their heisting activities. Their headquarters supplies guns based on the rank of the employee.
