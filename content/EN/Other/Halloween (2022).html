The Halloween Event is here! 🎃 To activate the event, a player must use a special (Event item) weather stick called "spook stick" obtained via Prospecting. spooky weather will occur upon using it and zombies will rise from the underworld to attack those who infiltrate their territories across the map. Be prepared! Stock up on ammo and firearms to take down the undead so you can loot their corpses for prizes and defeat Dr. Iridium!


== Getting Started ==

  • During the spooky weather, an automatic outbreak zone occurs at a random time.
  • Follow the outbreak zone location marked on your map.
  • Defeat all the zombies.
  • Loot their bodies by pressing the E Key.

Once all the bodies are looted, you will be notified that you have completed the current zone and are ready to receive the next one.
For the time being, you can refill your ammunition or wait for the next outbreak event to occur.

Pigs (Company) and <a data-p

"Hunter">Hunter Job are Not allowed to participate in this Event. Be sure to have any other job equipped for the event to work

info

Obtaining a Weapon

Ways to obtain firearms are as follows:

  • Cleaning a Firearm, obtained via Prospecting or purchased off the Marketplace.
  • Easter Egg weapons.
  • Using Halloween Candy to buy Firearms at the Ammunition Market around the map.

Goodies and Prizes

The following items can be obtained when looting zombie corpses:

  • Halloween Candy (Spooky Treats that you can trade for various items)
  • Zombie Meat (It's edible food)
  • Pristine Trick or Treat Bag
  • Ragged Trick or Treat Bag
  • Used Trick or Treat Bag
  • Worn Trick or Treat Bag
