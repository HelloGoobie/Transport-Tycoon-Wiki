Activities

Dr Iridium Boss Fight

Dr. Iridium is a special Halloween 2025 boss encounter. Players will need to work together to bring him down due to his scaling health and deadly abilities.

  • Minion Spawn: Summons minions that can one-shot you if not defeated quickly.
  • Shockwave: Emits periodic shockwaves that knock players into the air.
  • Explosive Barrage: Calls down a barrage of explosions marked by red circles on the ground.
  • Scaling Health: Dr. Iridium’s health scales with the number of players online at 5,000 HP per player
  • Loot Eligibility: You must deal enough damage to qualify for loot rewards.

Dr. Iridium can drop the following:

  • Normal Halloween 2025 Loot Bag
  • Rare Halloween 2025 Loot Bag

Outbreak Zones

In the event where you're unable to reach a zombie or the zombie is stuck under a building/terrain, you must abort the mission by pressing M → Stop Job while you are near or in the outbreak zone. Otherwise you will be stuck on that zone, even if any future zones are activated.

info

Be prepared! Stock up on ammo and firearms to take down the undead so you can loot their corpses for prizes and defeat Dr. Iridium!

  • An automatic outbreak zone Zombie Breakout Zone occurs at a random time.
  • Follow the outbreak zone location marked on your map.
  • Defeat all the zombies. (They can easily one shot you if they hit you)
  • Some of the zombies do have carbines. (Marked by their different icon inside the zone)
  • Loot their bodies by pressing the E Key. Any zombies killed with vehicles will not give any loot.

Once all the bodies are looted, you will be notified that you have completed the current zone and are ready to receive the next one.
For the time being, you can refill your ammunition or wait for the next outbreak event to occur.
At the current moment Zombie Meat cannot be used for anything but eating.
Don't drive in these zones as you will be griefing other players, although you can't see their zombies, you are still running them over, ruining their gameplay.

Michael Myers

Be on the lookout for Michael Myers, he has been spotted in San Andreas!

When Michael Myers is nearby, you will receive a notification just above your minimap stating "A scream can be heard nearby".
Kill him (running over with vehicle does not count), then press E to loot his body to get a reward.
If you drive too far away, a notification will state "A scream has faded away". This means he has despawned.
Just like the regular zombies he can only be seen on your screen and not on others.

Trick or Treating

Between the hours of 8 PM and 6 AM (in-game) you can go to doors and press E to trick or treat.
Each door has the chance to give candy or a Halloween Loot Bag.
Be warned that some people will not be happy with you knocking on their door and will come out with a gun. (Be prepared!)
The best area is Grove Street and Appartments nearby that area. Mirror Park is also a good area.

Doors can only be looted once per night, no need to relog to go trick or treating again just wait for 8pm again!

Jack O' Lanterns

In order to farm Jack O' Lanterns, you will need at least level 30 <a data-p

"Farming">Farming.

Head to the Job Center to become a Farmer.
Head to O Neil's Pumpkin Farm O Neil Pumpkin Farm in Grapeseed to purchase Pumpkin seeds. For as long as you stay in the buy zone, you will continue buying 5 seeds for $10K ($10,000) every second.
Plant Pumpkin seeds in any Field you own. They will take 180 minutes (3 hours) to fully grow.
Wait for the pumpkins to be ready to be harvested. Fertilizer can be used to speed this up.
After harvesting, take the raw pumpkins to the Union Grain Inc Union Grain and convert your raw pumpkins into pumpkins per 5.
You can now take your pumpkins and a Gut Knife (Stat Trak Knives don't work) to O Neil's Pumpkin Farm O Neil Pumpkin Farm and craft Jack O' Lanterns. 1 pumpkin and one Gut Knife give 1 Jack O' Lantern. The Gut Knife is returned upon use.

Jack 'O Lanterns can be used at the Weapons Market.

Obtaining a Weapon

Ways to obtain firearms are as follows:

  • Cleaning a Firearm, obtained via Prospecting or purchased from the Marketplace.
  • Easter Egg weapons (As the name implies these are secret and are not shared on the Wiki)
  • Using Halloween Candy or Jack O' Lanterns to buy Firearms at the Weapons Market Market (Weapons) around the map.
