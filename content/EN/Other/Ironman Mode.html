--- title: Ironman Mode ---

Ironman Mode is a toggleable feature that disables the Double EXP benefits and disables player to player trading. The Marketplace is still accessible.
Players in this mode are automatically forced into Passive Mode, everywhere.

This mode can be toggled via M►Player Options►Toggle Ironman Mode

Benefits

At least one known benefit of Ironman mode is the ability to only receive decrypted SD-Cards that have not yet been unlocked, unless all cards are unlocked already.
