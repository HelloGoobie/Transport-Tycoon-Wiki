Jerry Can Empty is an item that is purchased from Generic Shop 27Market (Gas Station) and can be exported at the Bristols Fuel Storage. It is part of the Petrochemical Trucking job.

Can also be filled up with 20 liters of fuel (Diesel/Petrol) at any Gas Station Fuel 20 and used manually to refill a vehicle.
