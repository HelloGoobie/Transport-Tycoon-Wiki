--- title: Jester ---

Jester may refer to:

  • The Jester, a vehicle purchasable from the Car Dealer under the Sports section.
  • The Jester Racecar, a vehicle unlocked after using an Upgrade Kit (Jester) on the Jester. This also costs $500,000 ($500k) and requires at least level 10 in the Street Racer skill.
  • The Jester Super Secret, a vehicle unlocked after using an Upgrade Kit (Jester classic) on the Jester Classic. The Jester Classic can be found in the Sports Classics section.
