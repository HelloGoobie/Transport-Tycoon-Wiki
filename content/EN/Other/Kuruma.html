Kuruma may refer to:

  • The Kuruma, available in the Sports section of the Car Dealership.
  • The Kuruma T/A Mk1, obtained after using a Upgrade Kit (Kuruma) at the Sports Upgrade Station.
  • The Kuruma T/A Mk2, obtained after using a Upgrade Kit (Kuruma Mk1) at the Sports Upgrade Station.
