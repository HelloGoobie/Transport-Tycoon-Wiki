Information

LITE Transportation is a lightweight gamemode with a simple goal in mind; trucking.

Taking on inspiration from the Truck Simulator series, and similar modifications to previous Grand Theft Auto titles, it offers simple and easy-to-understand gameplay.

The gamemode is running ("less than") the bare minimum needed for a FiveM server, and a custom framework. No extra content is streamed, only making use of the content provided by the game.

How to join

Method Nº1: Search "[LITE]" in FiveM servers search bar

Method Nº2: Launch FiveM, open console [F8] and paste: connect dgpvx3 or connect lite.tycoon.community

Method Nº3: Use external link via your browser: fivem://connect/dgpvx3 or http://connect.tycoon.community/

How to play

Getting started, you are put in a van. Your only goal at this point is to locate a nearby Delivery LITE-Delivery1 and complete it. Upon completing a couple jobs, you should be able to afford a better vehicle. To buy your first vehicle, head to one of the Vehicle Dealerships LITE-Vehicle Dealership. Press E on the spot to open the shop menu and select your wanted vehicle. New (more expensive) vehicles will increase your payout from deliveries.

Ingame you can press F6 to open Settings menu or F7 to open Tips menu.
The only available command on the server is /respawn - your vehicle will be respawned at the nearest Service Station LITE-Service Station fully fueled and repaired, but keep in mind that it will also cancel your current delivery.

Features

Partnered Server

By completing deliveries on [LITE] Transportation, you will gain some rewards (Trucking BXP and Skills Tokens) on Transport Tycoon servers.

Tebex Bonuses

You can get additional bonuses to speed up your progression on the server from the Tebex Shop.

Dynamic Jobs

All players have access to the same pool of jobs, no players are given a different set of jobs at any point in time. Upon completing a job, it is no longer possible to start it again, and a new job is generated. Several players can do the same job, even after it is no longer available, as long as it was started before a player completed it.

Progression

Progression is all about upgrading your equipment and purchasing new vehicles. The bigger and better the vehicle is, the more you get paid per job. Completed deliveries are tallied, and at certain milestones you will be shown more information about each available job, making it easier for you to determine which jobs you wish to do.

- Deliveries Specifics

  • LITE-Delivery1 Delivery - 0 deliveries required
  • LITE-Delivery2 Short Distance Delivery - 15 deliveries required
  • LITE-Delivery3 Long Distance Delivery - 25 deliveries required
  • LITE-Delivery4 High Value Delivery - 50 deliveries required
  • LITE-Delivery5 Extremely Valuable Delivery - 100 deliveries required

Quick Travel

Quick Travel allows you to quickly move around the map. It is free while you are not currently on a job. Possible destinations will be shown on your radar while in the quick travel menu. Keep in mind that the travel cost might be quite pricey while on the job.

For quick travel you can use:

  • Airports LITE-Airport,
  • Seaports LITE-Seaport,
  • Train Stations LITE-Train Station.

Fuel

All vehicles consume fuel over time, this can be replenished at any Fuel Station LITE-Fuel Station or at a Service Station LITE-Service Station. These are all marked on the map. Fuel is optional, and can be disabled by the player in the Settings menu found by pressing F6.

No-collision

Player to player collision is disabled at all times, so other players cannot interfere with your gameplay.
