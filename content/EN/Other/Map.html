--- title: Map ---

Transport Tycoon uses a custom map with added locations such as new airports and race tracks. A great tool for viewing the map can be found at ttmap.eu.

Features

The Transport Tycoon map has many features such as:

  • Aircraft Dealership
  • Businesses
  • Car Dealers
  • City Hall
  • Clothing Stores
  • Experience boosts
  • Fountain of Wealth
  • Garages
  • Grand Exchange
  • Houses
  • Job Centers
  • Fishing Store
  • Marketplace
  • Self Storages
  • Trucking Locations
