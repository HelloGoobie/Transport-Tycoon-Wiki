--- title: Money ---

Money or Cash is the currency used in Transport Tycoon it is used for crafting in-game items and trading with other players. It can be obtained through numerous sources, such as doing jobs and selling items. Eventually investing in businesses will earn you money passively.

Acquiring Money

Working

  • Doing most jobs in general is a reliable source of direct income. The amount of money earned is determined based on your job & equipment level. In general: the higher they are, the more money you get.

You may resort to other means of obtaining money in which you can increase your earnings (in)directly via:

  • Selling Items on the Marketplace Marketplace 24, it's a quick way to earn cash. Be aware of the 5% service fee. Don't always go off of the estimated marketplace value, if you are unsure on how your price things, asking in the chat will probably get some people to help you. The value is easy to manipulate and is sometimes much higher than existing offers on the Marketplace. You also lose another 10% once the offer has been sold.
  • Buying and investing in Businesses Business Sale, a long-term process which generates passive income but will only earn you profit in the long run.
  • Visiting the Fountain of WealthFountain will give you an amount of cash donated by other players to get you started.
  • Using various codes will also give new players a head start.

Tips

Beginner Tips

While there are many ways to obtaining money, here are a few tips that may help you decide how you can make money efficiently.

  • Garbage Collector, Train Conductor and EMS are some of the best jobs for newer players. This is due to their low starting equipment costs and decent to good scaling, especially for EMS due to the streak system.
  • Trucking: One of the highest paid end-game jobs. Process and deliver goods. Advanced routes will unlock upon leveling up. They will earn you more, but will require better and more expensive Trailers.
    • Will be used in combination with Vehicle Trunks
    • It is recommended to increases your Strength level. Strength increases your inventory capacity, which will become a priority for jobs that require you to transport heavy goods.
    • Don't be afraid to spend cash on better Trailers for trucking. They will allow you to be more efficient over time as you will be able to transport more goods at the same time.
  • Doing side activities such as Prospecting, Treasure Hunting or EXP Boost Hunting and then selling the items you receive to other players.

Advanced Tips

  • In the process of doing Trucking, It can be more worth selling the products to other Players via Self Storages rather than exporting them to the NPC/AI. Given that some materials, such as Concrete are used in advanced trucking which increases its sale value when trading is applied.
  • Producing and selling the Bonus EXP items you get from doing Jobs to other players is also a really good way of making money. This is most effective for more advanced players with the Cargo Pilot Perk activated and high Base EXP due to multiple Experience Boosts.
    • This is especially effective for Jobs that have active streaks, since those jobs give very high amounts of base exp.
  • In general jobs with streaks, they do give more money and exp at high streak, with some jobs not even having a maximum streak.
