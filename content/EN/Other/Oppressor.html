--- title: Oppressor ---

Oppressor may refer to:

  • Oppressor MK1
    • Is an Upgrade Kit that requires the player to own the Ruffian bike which can be purchased from the Car Dealer under Motorcycles section. Take both the Ruffian bike and the upgrade kit (Ruffian) to the Market (Alien Workshop) to upgrade it into Oppressor MK1.
  • Oppressor F/S
    • Is the next upgraded version of the Oppressor MK1 which requires the player to own the Oppressor MK1 + 100 Spaceship Parts, It is also upgraded at the Alien Workshop.

  • Oppressor MK2

    • Can be purchased at the Car Dealer under Premium vehicles section. It cost $100.000.000.000 (100 bil).
