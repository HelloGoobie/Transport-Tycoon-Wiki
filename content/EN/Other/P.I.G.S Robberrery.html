Overview

The Pandas Illicit Gains Syndicate (P.I.G.S.) is a robbery job that provides waypoint-marked locations to rob. Upon arriving at the location, follow the prompt to ready up and begin the heist. Heists can be completed either solo or with a group.

  • Once you receive the P.I.G.S. Robbery Job from the location (Level 30 Strength required):*
  • Head inside and approach the bar to either join or create a party. The party host can select a difficulty tier between 1 and 7.
  • After selecting "Start Heist," a location will be provided. Once all players are ready, weapons will be given and waves of enemies will begin spawning.
  • You gain EXP in three skills: Hunting, Business, and Player.
  • If you die during the heist, it will fail and a new location will be assigned.

Rewards

XP is distributed as follows: Hunting - 40% Business - 40% Player - 20%

The amount of XP awarded is based on your streak (Click here to see XP formulas). This increases depending on your heist tier and streak.

Max streak that can be gained is 1000 (1k).

Money distribution for a solo player at a streak of 1 can average between $9000 and $40000, dependent on the tier of heist and streak. (For more details see The money formula that is used in the calculations.
