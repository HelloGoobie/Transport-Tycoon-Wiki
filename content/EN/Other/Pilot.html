--- title: Pilot ---

Pilot may refer to:

  • Airline Pilot, transport passengers to and from various airports
  • Cargo Pilot, transport goods to and from various airports
  • Helicopter Pilot, transport passengers to and from various locations
  • Aerial Firefighter, extinguish forest fires with a water bomber plane
  • Leisure Pilot, a job that allows you to fly around in your spare time with certain aircraft
