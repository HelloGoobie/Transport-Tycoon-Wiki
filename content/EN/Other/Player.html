--- title: Player ---

Player is one of the skills in Transport Tycoon. The Player skill is a general indication of your progress in the server, as most activities will earn you Experience in the relevant activity and your Player skill.

To view your Player Level, Press M → Skills.
To close the interface, press M → Skills again.
