Premium Fish Meat is an item that can be obtained from Fishing. Its sole purpose is to sell to one of the four Fish Importers across the map. It is currently not worth it to exlusively farm Premium Fish Meat at the Paleto Cove Fishing Zone.

Obtaining

Several fishes can give Premium Fish Meat instead of regular Fish Meat. The Pacific Cove Fishing Zone exclusively gives Premium Fish Meat.

  • Goby (Level 25+)
    • Angelfish (Level 25+)
    • Frogfish (Level 25+)
