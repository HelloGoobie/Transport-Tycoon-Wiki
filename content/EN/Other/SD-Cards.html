--- title: SD-Cards ---

(Encrypted) SD cards are mainly used to unlock better or alternative routes and recipes for the Trucking job.
(Encrypted) SD cards were added in the November 10th, 2021 update.

Ways To Obtain SD Cards

  1. Prospecting - To prospect, simply go in game and find a white circle on the map, and press E to start prospecting. (Make sure you own a prospecting scanner.)
  2. Daily Chests - This is another way of getting a SD Card. Daily Chests are obtainable automatically when you log in to the server every day.
  3. Marketplace - This is the easiest way to obtain encrypted SD Cards & decrypted SD Card Recipes.
    • SD cards can not be traded (i.e. can not be given to people), but are obtainable through the Marketplace.

How do SD Cards work

  1. When you got an Encrypted SD Card, you need to keep them in your Inventory.
  2. Then go to the FIB Building.
    • In the entrance area you will see a green Marker next to the desk where you can decrypt SD-Cards (Start Scan), see what Secrets you have unlocked already (View learned Secrets) and check your current scan (Check Scan).
  3. Choose "Start Scan" and choose the decrypting method.
    • You can choose between the following options:
      • Slow Scan (48h) = FREE
      • Regular Scan (12h) = $200,000,000
      • Fast Scan (4h) = $1,000,000,000
      • Instant scan (one time) = $1,000,000,000
  4. After the scanning time, you should have the Decrypted SD Card in your Inventory.
    • IF NOT: Go to the FIB Building again and choose "Check Scan"
  5. If you want to learn the secret recipe of the SD Card you got, choose it in your Inventory and hit "Unlock secret".
    • You have learned the secret recipe now and the SD Card will disappear.
