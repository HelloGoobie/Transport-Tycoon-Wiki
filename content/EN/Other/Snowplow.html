Getting Started

  1. Go to one of the 3 snowplow spawn points marked with icon on your map.
    1. The first and closest to city waypoint is near los santos airport.
    2. The next spawn point is near the trucking filtering facility.
    3. The last spawn point is in Paleto bay.
  2. Exit your vehicle and walk into the yellow circle to spawn a Snowplow.
  3. Enable salting by pressing the E key.
  4. Start driving around and cover as much fresh roads as you can.
  5. When you are out of salt, go to any of the Salt Refill stations marked with on the map to refill.

More Information

  • As you drive around, be sure not to drive over the same spot again in a row since it has to "refill" with snow.
    • We recommend taking the highway since it provides a direct loop around the map.
  • You will collect Snowplowing Tokens: as you drive around. These tokens can subsequently be used to purchase new items at a limited-time store located at City Hall.
  • When you drive nearby other snowplows, you will receive a Convoy Bonus which will be applied to your earned Cash and Snowplowing Tokens. This bonus increases depending on how many other snowplows are nearby.
    • When the convoy bonus reaches 200%, a special chat title will unlock for all convoy participants to use.
  • Activating salting gives you additional Snowplowing Tokens. The quantity of salt you have is computed as a percentage of 100. A counter will show next to your in-game mini map, allowing you to see how much time you have remaining.
  • It is better to drive as fast as you can, as it allows you to cover the most distance.
  • Players in the Top 10 positions on the job's leaderboard will have access to a special chat title.
