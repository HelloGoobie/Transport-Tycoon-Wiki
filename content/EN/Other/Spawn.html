--- title: Spawn ---

Spawn may refer to:

Player Spawn:

  • Spawn Menu, It pops up once every time you load into the server. It allows you to choose a spawn point within your owned Businesses, House or a Faction.
  • City Hall, the spawn area when you first join the server.
  • Respawn, Instance of respawning when a player dies.

Vehicle Spawn:

  • Semi Spawners, a set of locations where you can spawn vehicles.
  • Garage, spawn owned vehicles.
