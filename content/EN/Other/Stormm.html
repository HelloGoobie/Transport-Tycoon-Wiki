--- title: Stormm ---

About

Stormm is a Transport Tycoon staff member and is the Community Director of the server. He is the 4th Community Director of the server, and has been since January 9th, 2023, following DetectiveFabian stepping down. Joining Transport Tycoon in February 2018, Stormm was previously the Head Adminstrator from 2021-2023, Moderator from 2018-2021, and Support from June-September 2018. Outside of the Staff Team, he was also the Co-CEO of Imperial Airlines from 2020-2022, contributed heavily to the original Tycoon Wiki, and is responsible for several of the in-game Transport Tycoon themed vehicle liveries.

Server Work

As the Community Director, Stormm is responsible for managing the Staff Team and situations within the whole community. New staff members are all selected and interviewed by the Community Director as they proceed into the staff team. The Community Director is one of the three head staff roles, in between Server Owner and Head Administrator, and when there is no Head Administrator the Community Director assumes the responsibilities of both roles.

As a vehicle artist he has made liveries for Imperial Airlines, CollinsCo, F.R. LLC, Rockwell Corp, Airline Pilot (Miljet,Q400,787,A330,Concorde), Cargo Pilot (A350), PostOp (JU-52,EMB-390,P3), and several other smaller projects.

Personal

Stormm is a 22 year old student currently studying Film and is a semi-professional photographer. Previously studying business, Stormm holds a diploma in Management.
