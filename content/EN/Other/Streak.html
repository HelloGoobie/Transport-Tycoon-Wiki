--- title: Streak ---

The revamped streak system, introduced on 8 May 2025. It allows players to earn more Experience or other rewards the higher their streak is.

Streaks reset after the clock goes past 00:00 UTC three times and you did not complete at least one objective/delivery, so for example:

You complete a EMS mission at 11:00 pm UTC, there is 1 hour left until midnight, then another 48'' until it is midnight UTC for the second time, meaning you only have a total of49 hours'' to keep your streak.

Jobs that already benefit from the streak system:

EMS

As you deliver patients to the hospital, you build up a streak. At every drop off you earn more Experience and EXP Tokens for every patient dropped off. Below is a picture of a graph of how EMS XP (Ground), EMS XP (Helicopter), Heli XP (Helicopter) and Mobile ER (EMS XP) scales up after each drop off.

Firefighter

There are no set streak thresholds, the exp earned is dependent on 3 variables. The region in which you have completed the call-out (tier, this can be a number between 1 and 6 and always is 6 at level 100+), the difficulty of a fire (difficulty, this is a random number between 1 and 5) and your streak (with a maximum of 1000).

This also applies to the amount of money you get, but this gets calculated through a different formula. The amount of BXP items you get is calculated by the raw exp formula.

The formula for raw exp: (tier * 5 + 7 + difficulty) * (1 + 0.2 * ((streak - 1) / 100) ^ 1.5)

The formula for money: (tier + difficulty + (streak / 10)) * 5000

RTS

As you complete RTS deliveries, you increase your streak by 1, this will increase the amount of exp you gain per delivery.

The streak increases rewards linearly and starts at 7.5 EXP and 30 000$. The RTS streak has currently no maximum.

The increase is +0.075 EXP and 300$ per delivery

(This is not yet formally confirmed, but the result of the communities testing)

Cabbie

As you complete Cabbie callouts, you increase your streak by 1, this will increase the amount of exp you gain per dropoff.

The Cabbies streak starts at 6 exp. The exp for a dropoff is dependent on the distance of the delivery, the current streak and if you manage to deliver in time.

The formula for raw exp: (distance) * ((100 + streak)/100) * (time bonus)

Where distance can have the value 6 exp for deliveries that are less than 3500m away and 7.5 exp for ones that are further away and the time bonus is a *2 Multiplier if you manage to deliver in time.

HUD


The progress of your current streak can be seen in this tab under identity in your M menu.

If one number is visible it shows your current streak and highest streak in one. If two numbers are shown, the one on the left is your current streak and the one on the right was your highest streak.
