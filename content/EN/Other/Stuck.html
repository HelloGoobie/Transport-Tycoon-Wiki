--- title: Stuck ---

Being stuck is an unfortunate given in this economy. Being stuck means you are not being efficient. Efficiency is key in the world of Transport Tycoon.

Ideally, you'd want to become unstuck. Getting unstuck is of the highest priority.

Listed below are several scenarios and their possible solutions.

Stuck on Foot

If you managed to get stuck on foot, lodged in an object for example, your best bet is to relog. Upon relogging, you will be given a list of possible locations to teleport to. If you don't have any teleports unlocked, you will have to make a staff ticket and request to be moved. This can be done by typing /report, followed by your message. For example: /report I am stuck between a rock and a hard place, can someone please help me out of my misery?

Stuck in the Middle of the Ocean

The ocean is vast and scary. Despite this, escaping the ocean is quite simple. The server will automatically respawn you on land if you manage to drown. It might sound morbid, but go ahead and taste the salty depths of the ocean. Before you know it, your feet will be touching land.

If instead you want to create an impromptu adventure out of this scenario, you could also spawn a jetski (B→Spawn Jet Ski) and sail the seven seas to land. Don't pretend you've discovered new land though, you're not a navigator!

Stuck in a vehicle

Vehicle is Stuck on an Object

If your vehicle is stuck on an object, try to lift the hydraulics. Hold LeftAlt, press the right mouse button and find the 'Lift Hydraulics' sphere. Once the sphere is pink, try moving back and forth and wiggle to either side. Eventually the wheels will pop up, giving you more vertical space to wiggle yourself free of this doom scenario.

Vehicle is Upside Down

If your vehicle is upside down, you should see a prompt appear in the bottom right, telling you what key to press to flip over the car. In the unfortunate case you have already exited the vehicle, your only hope is to attempt to flip it over by colliding into it with another vehicle. If that is not a possibility, you'll have to Autopark/Zdelete the vehicle and respawn it at an appropriate Garage once more.
