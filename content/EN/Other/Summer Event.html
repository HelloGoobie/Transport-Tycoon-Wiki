Selling your Ice Cream

After delivering your Ice Cream, you've now earned yourself some, which you will have to sell.
In order to do that, you'll have to get yourself an Ice Cream truck (Mr Tasty / Mr Tastier)

You'll have to head to one of the following locations (other locations exist but eh who cares right ?)

Near Weazel News HQ:



Now that you have found yourself the correct vehicle, simply park on the street and turn off your engine (either via 8 menu or Alt menu) and wait for a customer to arrive, press E and complete the QTE to serve them.
Each Ice Cream sold will count towards unlocking event titles, you will also obtain yourself some Summer Chests, these contains some interesting stuff !

Congrats, you're now a certified Ice Cream seller !
== More Informations ==

- There is a teleporter that allows you to instantly go back to the pickup point but it costs 10 Ice Creams per trip.

Teleporter location:



- Once you start opening Summer Chests, you will easily earn Mr Tasty / Mr Tastier spawn cards, so you probably won't need to go the spawner anymore.

- Failing a QTE while selling won't make you lose any Ice Cream.

- When spawning a delivery truck, a ~5 minutes cooldown is applied before you can spawn an other one.
