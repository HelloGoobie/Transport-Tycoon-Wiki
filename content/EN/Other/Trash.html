--- title: Trash ---

Trash may refer to:

  • Trucking:
    • Recycled Trash, obtained from the Recycling Center Trucking Seller and used at the Sorting Facility Blip Factory 3 to make Trucking Products.
  • Garbage Collector:
    • The Trashmaster truck, obtainable in the Car Dealer.
  • Beach Cleaner:
    • Trash Item, obtained by cleaning beaches and is used at the Recycling Plant Recycling Plant to craft Treasure Chests.
