--- title: Treasure ---

Treasure Chests Treasure are chests that spawn individually whenever someone levels up in a skill. They can also randomly spawn in bulk. The chests can easily be found because of their icon on the map and their high-rising yellow beam.

Ways to obtain Treasure Chests

While Treasure Chests are obtained simply by passing through the high-rising yellow beam, there are other ways to obtain them too and are listed below:

  • Beach Cleaning will give you Trash as a sub-product which can be saved up to create low/medium/high and ultra-grade Treasure chests at the Recycling Plant Recycling Plant up North in Sandy Shores.
  • Low and Medium-grade Chests can also be obtained by donating to the Fountain of Wealth. $50k for low-grade chest & $250k for medium-grade chest.
  • Exclusively, Ultra-grade Treasure Chest can be purchased from the Market(Premium/VIP) for 10 Skill Level Tokens.
  • Can also be purchased from the Marketplace or traded with other players.

Treasure Key

To open a treasure chest, one will need a Treasure Key, which is consumed when you open the chest. These keys can be found from the same high-rising yellow beams or:

  • Received via breaking down a medium, high or ultra grade chest.
  • Prospecting (Legendary 0.09%) (Rare 0.5%).
  • Open Treasure Chest [Low-grade] (5.55%).
  • Buying it from a player or Marketplace.

If you have a treasure key and a treasure chest, go to M → Inventory → Your chest → Open to open the treasure chest.

After interacting with the chest, it will grant an item (see treasure contents below) and will subsequently apply a cooldown of a few seconds to prevent people from farming chests too rapidly.

Daily Chests

A Treasure Chest [Daily] can be obtained automatically when you log in to the server every day.

  • There's also a 4.54% chance a Treasure Chest [Daily] contains another Daily Chest when opened.

Rewards

  • High grade treasure
  • Medium grade treasure
  • Low grade treasure
  • Treasure keys

All items can be sold on the Marketplace
