Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Tycoon Gaming Wiki. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, and protection for our users.

Data Collection

Account Information: We collect usernames and passwords solely for the purpose of creating and managing user accounts. No email or additional personal identifying information is required. User Contributions: Contributions to the wiki are publicly visible and associated with the user's account.

Use of Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for account management and user authentication. We do not use this information for marketing or any other purposes.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information is not shared with third parties, except as required by law. User contributions are publicly accessible and may be used by other users.

Cookies and Tracking

The Tycoon Gaming Wiki uses cookies as part of the MediaWiki software. These cookies are essential for website functions such as user session management. We do not have control over these cookies and do not use them for tracking purposes. Users can control cookie settings through their browser settings.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

User Rights

Users have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of their personal data. For such requests, please contact us using the details provided below.

Data Retention

We store collected data indefinitely, as long as the server hosting the data remains operational. Users can request the deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy as necessary. Any significant changes will be notified on the Wiki.

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact Glitchdetector. (Discord Name: glitchdetector)
