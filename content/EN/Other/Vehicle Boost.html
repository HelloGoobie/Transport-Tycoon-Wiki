Vehicle Boost is a vehicle modification that can be applied to vehicles which can increase the top speed of your vehicle. It is served as a temporary speed boost to vehicles until the vehicle is despawned. There are currently 10%, 15%, 25% and 30% vehicle boosts available for vehicles.




Obtaining

To use Vehicle Boost, your vehicle must have Turbo installed. This can be done in LS Customs

info

Multiple methods in which you can apply a Vehicle Boost to your vehicle are as follows:

Faction

  • Certain Factions offer temporary Vehicle Boosts (Vehicle Boost Vendor) Vehicle Booster as a Faction Perk, which can be purchased by the Faction President and Faction Officers, offering 15% or 25% Boost at the cost of $150.00k & $250.00k respectively when applied to vehicles (per use).
  • Alternatively, Garage Speed Boost Faction Perk automatically applies a free 10% boost to your vehicle when the vehicle is spawned within the Faction Garage.

On the Map

  • 15% Vehicle Booster variants can also be found in 3 different locations around the map. The cost per use is $150.00k.

Racing Perk

  • The last and currently highest boost available is 30%, which is applied via obtaining and enabling the Racing Perk. This type of boost can be enabled/disabled from the perks list on your character's Phone. When enabled, it auto-applies to all owned vehicles that are spawned from a garage.

Information

  • While other Vehicle boosters can be used to boost both you and other player's vehicles, the 30% boost type is only applied upon spawning your owned vehicle and can not be applied to other players if they don't own the racing perk.
  • Vehicle boosts can also be applied to Planes, allowing a slight boost while taxiing. however, this makes it harder to navigate and steer in the process while offering no actual boost in the air.
  • Starting a race in a boosted vehicle will automatically remove the percentage of the boost at the beginning of the race.

Trivia

  • Staff Vehicles can have a 50% speed boost.
    • Admins and above can add a custom boost amount.
  • During events (in the event server), all player vehicles can be boosted (simultaneously) far beyond 50% by staff members.
