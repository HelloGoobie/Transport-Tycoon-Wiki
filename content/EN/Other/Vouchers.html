--- title: Vouchers ---

Vouchers may refer to:

  • Company vouchers, an item obtained by doing company work. They can be given to a manager in return for money.
  • Vouchers from mining:
    • Copper Ore Voucher
    • Iron Ore Voucher
    • Roxite Ore Voucher
  • The old skill vouchers, who used to give experience at the Grand Exchange.
