The Clothing Store is the place where you can customize your character's appearance or choose between a variety of models from different kinds of work clothing & pedestrian outfits.

  • There are a total of 19 Clothing stores around the map.
  • Once you are done customizing your character, you can save your outfit by going to: M → Player Options → Wardrobe → Save.
  • To delete an outfit from the Wardrobe menu, highlight the outfit that you have previously saved then press Left Arrow key to delete it.
  • There's no limit to how many outfits you can store in your Wardrobe.
Keep in mind that upon changing your outfit from the Wardrobe menu, you may lose any equipments/weapons you have on your character.
To remove the default white shirt on your character, choose the following: Hands #16, Shirt #16, Jacket #16 then you can modify the shirt/jacket that you like without it overlapping!
