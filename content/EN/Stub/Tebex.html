Transport Tycoon has a web store, where benefits can be bought in exchange for real life currency.

It can be accessed by typing /shop in-game, or by navigating to the Transport Tycoon web store.

Listed below are all options available on the web store. Refer to the web store for up-to-date information.

In-Game

Bonuses

Double EXP Ticket

This bonus is only available on your current server. Once the party starts, it will remain active on that server. In the event of another Double EXP Party being active, yours will be queued up and is started once the previous Party or Parties has finished.

The Double EXP Ticket allows you to start a Double Experience Party on your server that will last for 30 minutes!

Everyone gains 100% more base experience while the party is active.

Treasure Master Key

A reusable item that allows you to open an indefinite amount of Treasure Chests. It can not be traded or trashed.

Mechanic Toolbox

A consumable item which grants additional Mechanic EXP by 50% for 1 hour.

Extra Business Stack

Permanently increases your businesses' maximum collection limit by 1. (16 by default)

By default, your businesses will collect revenue for up to 48 hours (2 days / 16 cycles).

TL;DR: Allows your businesses to collect revenue for another 3 hours (per purchase). Each cycle is 3 hours, meaning 8 cycles will occur every real day.

Extra Business Day

Permanently increases your businesses' maximum collection limit by 8. (16 by default)

By default, your businesses will collect revenue for up to 48 hours (2 days / 16 cycles).

Each cycle is 3 hours, meaning 8 cycles will occur every real day.

TL;DR: Allows your businesses to collect revenue for another 24 hours (per purchase)

Customizations

You need to be the Faction President of the Faction you are currently in!

Normal rules for Faction name and tag applies. We will not provide refunds.

Customize the license plate on your vehicle!

Restrictions:

Upgrades

Speed Trap EMP

Nearby Speed Traps are disabled, meaning you will never be caught again.

Pre-paid Autopass

Using a pre-paid autopass means you will pass any Toll Plaza for free, regardless of what car you are driving.

Instant Respawn

Dying in water will always put you back on land.

You no longer need Pills to re-spawn where you died. The fade-out effect when re-spawning will no longer occur.

This perk replaces the existing "No hunger / thirst" perk

Old description: Completely disable the need to eat and drink!

Fuel System

Quality of life changes to the fuel system.

You can upgrade to Disable Fuel System at a discounted rate if you already own Free Fuel

Free Fuel

No longer pay for fuel, you can refuel for free!

Disable fuel system

Disable the need to maintain fuel in your vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions repeatedly asked by the community, compiled for an easier time!

Are Business Stacks / Days permanent or one-time?

Business Stacks permanently increases your business' collection limit by 1 each. Meaning your businesses will collect revenue for an extra 3 hours before they must be collected. (Defaults to 16 stacks, or 48 hours)

Business Days do the exact same, but adds 8 each instead of just one.

When does the Double EXP Party start?

Double EXP Parties start instantly when the Double EXP Ticket is used in-game. It can be started at any time.
