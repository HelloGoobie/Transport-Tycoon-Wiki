Getting started

If this is your first time trucking, it's recommended to read the <a data-p

"Getting Started Guide">Getting Started Guide for essential basic information about usage of the vehicle trunk/storage and trucking in general.
  • Head to the Job Center Job Center job center and select the Trucker job.
  • Visit the Car Dealership Car Dealer car dealershop to purchase the Speedo Express or a combination of a semi-cab (truck) and trailer.
    • The Speedo Express is free and has a trunk capacity equal to your own inventory capacity from 30kg to 300kg.
  • Click on Getting Started Guide for First time truckers.

Vehicles

Trucking requires a semi-cab (truck) and trailer to store cargo in. Semi-cabs can be bought from a car dealership Car Dealer car dealershopor alternatively Semi Spawners can be spawned at a few locations around the map. Examples include LSIA, Sandy Shores, and LS Docks. Players that are short on cash can begin with the free Speedo Express. The trunk capacity of the Speedo scales with the players' Strength level.

Basic Commercial Routes

You start a trucking route by picking up cargo from one of the following cargo sellers. Go to the cargo seller to obtain your cargo, load as much as you can in your vehicle and then head to a Cargo Buyer that accepts the cargo to sell it. Logs, Recycled Trash, Toxic Waste, Recycled Electronics and Quarry Rubble are all part of more in-depth commercial trucking routes. Make sure to have a look at the Advanced Cargo Routes section.

Logging

Logging is unlocked at level 6, this is the first type of trucking where there are multiple steps in the process. You want to:

  • Pick up Logs from the Logging Camp.
  • Take the Logs to the Sawmill to turn them into Sawdust or Planks.
    • Both the Sawdust and the Planks can be sold at the LS Port Export or stored in Self Storages to be combined with other items.

Recycled Materials Base Process

At level 9 you will unlock Recycled Trash, this can be picked up from the Recycling Center. You want to:

  • Take the Recycled Trash to the Sorting Facility to obtain three materials from it:
    • Scrap Plastic can be exported from the McKenzie Export.
    • Scrap Aluminum and Scrap Tin can be refined at the LS Foundry.
      • Refined Aluminum and Refined Tin are exported from the LS Port Export.

From here, the process will evolve allowing more materials and new combinations of materials. The more steps the process has, the more you will earn from it. Item descriptions and exploration of the different locations should lead you on to new processes.

FAQs

How do I detach my trailer?

You can detach your Trailer by holding the H key. To re-attach your Trailer, press the H key when prompted, your Trailer will snap to the same orientation your Truck is facing.

How Can I customise my MK10-13

  1. Park your Trailer over LS Customs
  2. Detach your Trailer from your Truck
  3. Move your Truck away from LS Customs
  4. Head to the side over your trailer and press F to 'drive' the trailer
  5. Now use the Enter Key to enter LS Customs

How can I customise my MK14?

  1. Park your Trailer over LS Customs
  2. Detach your Trailer from your Truck
  3. Move your Truck away from LS Customs
  4. Go back to your Trailer and open your Trunk (see Getting Started Guide)
  5. The Trailer door will now be open, go into the back of your Trailer and press F to 'drive' the Trailer
  6. Now use the Enter key to enter LS Customs
