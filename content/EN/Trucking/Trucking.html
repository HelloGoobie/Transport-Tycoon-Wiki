Truckers are the backbone of Transport Tycoon. We’ve developed a complex web of cargo materials that allow you to simply sell one at a time, or collect and combine cargoes in multi-point routes for maximum profit!

Be sure to ask around for helpful guides developed by the community. Oh, and we also have a custom radio station! We offer TruckersFM,Simulator Radio and UpBeat Radio available in the left-bottom-center area of the radio wheel.

Getting started

If this is your first time trucking, it's recommended to read the Getting Started Guide for essential basic information about usage of the vehicle trunk/storage and trucking in general.
  • Head to the Job Center and select the Trucker job.
  • Visit the Car Dealership to purchase the Speedo Express or a combination of a semi-cab (truck) and trailer.
    • The Speedo Express is free and has a trunk capacity equal to your own inventory capacity from 30kg to 300kg.

Vehicles

Trucking requires a semi-cab (truck) and trailer to store cargo in. Semi-cabs can be bought from a car dealership or alternatively can be spawned at a few locations around the map. Examples include LSIA, Sandy Shores, and LS Docks. Players that are short on cash can begin with the free Speedo Express. The trunk capacity of the Speedo scales with the players' Strength level.

Basic Commercial Routes

You start a trucking route by picking up cargo from one of the following cargo sellers. Go to the cargo seller to obtain your cargo, load as much as you can in your vehicle and then head to a Cargo Buyer that accepts the cargo to sell it. Logs, Recycled Trash, Toxic Waste, Recycled Electronics and Quarry Rubble are all part of more in-depth commercial trucking routes. Make sure to have a look at the Advanced Cargo Routes section.

Logging

Logging is unlocked at level 6, this is the first type of trucking where there are multiple steps in the process. You want to:

  • Pick up Logs from the Logging Camp.
  • Take the Logs to the Sawmill to turn them into Sawdust or Planks.
    • Both the Sawdust and the Planks can be sold at the LS Port Export or stored in Self Storages to be combined with other items.

Recycled Materials Base Process

At level 9 you will unlock Recycled Trash, this can be picked up from the Recycling Center. You want to:

  • Take the Recycled Trash to the Sorting Facility to obtain three materials from it:
    • Scrap Plastic can be exported from the McKenzie Export.
    • Scrap Aluminum and Scrap Tin can be refined at the LS Foundry.
      • Refined Aluminum and Refined Tin are exported from the LS Port Export.

From here, the process will evolve allowing more materials and new combinations of materials. The more steps the process has, the more you will earn from it. Item descriptions and exploration of the different locations should lead you on to new processes.

There are Currently 3 Tycoon Premium Trucking Routes.

Concrete

Concrete is created from Quarry Rubble from the Quarry, Logs from a Logging Camp, Unfiltered Water from the Land Act Reservoir and Toxic Waste from the Elysian Island Waste Deposit. It can be sold at the Alta Construction Site.

Rebar

And then there is also the Rebar Route.

House

The House route uses every Advanced Trucking Route and all other Premium Trucking Routes and makes them to one ultimate Route. It requires Tycoon Premium and Lvl 100 Trucking to complete. Houses can be made at the House Construction Site.

Trucking Sub-types

Some trucking sub-routes require a trucking sub-job as well. These jobs can be acquired at the Trucking HQ . There are several trucking sub-jobs available:

  • Commercial
  • Refrigerated
  • Military
  • Petrochemical
  • Illegal
  • Master

It is also possible to change sub-job by using a Trucker's PDA. They can be obtained by buying it on the Marketplace, buying from a player and is also a possible reward from a Treasure Chest (Daily).
The master sub-job is obtained at 1,000,000 (1M) trucking experience, and allows you to see all (sub-)trucking transformers at once.

SD-Cards

SD Cards were added in the November 10th update in 2021. SD Cards are mainly used to unlock new routes for the Trucking job.

Ways To Obtain SD Cards

1. Prospecting (Prospecting) - To prospect simply go in game and find a white circle on the map, and press E to start prospecting. (Make sure you own a prospecting scanner.)

2. Daily Chests - This is another way of getting a SD Card. (To get your daily chests you simply login everyday)

3. Player To Player (Marketplace) - This is the easiest way to obtain encrypted SD Cards & decrypted SD Card Recipes. (Can only be done via marketplace as SD Cards are untradeable! )

How do SD Cards work

1. When you got a Encrypted SD Card you need to keep them in your Inventory.

2. Then go to the FIB Building. In the entrance area you will see a green Marker next to the Desk where you can decrypt SD-Cards (Start Scan), see what Secrets you have unlocked already (View learned Secrets) and check your current scan (Check Scan).

3. Choose "Start Scan" and choose the decrypting method. You can choose between the following options:

  • Instant Scan (0h) = $1,000,000,000 (ONE TIME USE)
  • Slow Scan (48h) = FREE
  • Regular Scan (12h) = $200,000,000
  • Fast Scan (4h) = $1,000,0000,000

4. After the scanning Time you should have the Decrypted SD Card in your Inventory. IF NOT: Go to the FIB Building again and choose "Check Scan"

5. If you want to learn the secret recipe of the SD Card you got choose it in your Inventory and hit "Unlock secret". You have learned the secret recipe now and the SD Card will disappear.

FAQs

How do I detach my trailer?

You can detach your Trailer by holding the H key. To re-attach your Trailer, press the H key when prompted, your Trailer will snap to the same orientation your Truck is facing.

How Can I customise my MK10-13

  1. Park your Trailer over LS Customs
  2. Detach your Trailer from your Truck
  3. Move your Truck away from LS Customs
  4. Head to the side over your trailer and press F to 'drive' the trailer
  5. Now use the Enter Key to enter LS Customs

How can I customise my MK14?

  1. Park your Trailer over LS Customs
  2. Detach your Trailer from your Truck
  3. Move your Truck away from LS Customs
  4. Go back to your Trailer and open your Trunk (see Getting Started Guide)
  5. The Trailer door will now be open, go into the back of your Trailer and press F to 'drive' the Trailer
  6. Now use the Enter key to enter LS Customs
