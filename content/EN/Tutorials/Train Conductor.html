Train Conductors Train emoji transport passengers around the map by rail. There are two different metro lines, one passenger train line and one freight train line available. As a level 1 Conductor, you will start with Metro and are then able to unlock Freight and Passenger Trains later on.

Metro / Subway Tram

  • Head to any Job Center Job Center job center (except for LifeInvader) and select the Train Conductor job. The smallest tier Metro requires level 1 conductor, which is what you start with.
  • Find one of the red metro train icons on the map, Metro Train located at LSIA or Davis street, then find the marker and press the indicated button to open the metro spawn menu.
  • Select a Metro train tier you can use.
  • Drive forward until the job prompt begins.
  • Drive your metro to all the stops indicated on your map Passenger Stop to complete the route.
  • You will earn Train Conductor Experience and Experience Tokens from each stop and for completing your route.
    • If your Train Conductor level is 100 or higher, you will instead receive extra Experience Tokens but no more regular Experience from Metro.

Passenger Train Conductor

  • Go to any Job Selector Job Center (except for LifeInvader) and select the Conductor job.
  • Drive to the train station near the Elysian Island docks, or the Paleto Bay train station just North of the Sawmill.
  • These train stations are marked by a red train icon Passenger Train on your map.
  • Find the floating orange marker on the terminal and press the indicated button to open the passenger train spawn menu.
  • Select your desired train based on your conductor level.
  • Drive your train forward to the terminal and begin your route.
  • Follow the tracks and stop at the designated stations Passenger Stop.
  • The route ends at Paleto Bay, where you can start the Southbound route that goes back to Los Santos again.
  • You will earn money, Experience and EXP Tokens from each stop and a bonus for completing a route.

In the city of Los Santos all passenger trains are slowed down automatically. As soon as you leave the city this restriction will be removed. It auto engages when you enter the city again.

Streaks

Streak system has been added to train conductor. The Train conductor streak uses a logarithm and increase less as the streak increase. You'll get more Experience Tokens, conductor Experience and more money per stop based on your streak.
