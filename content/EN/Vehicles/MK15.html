In order to obtain the Chernobog Toter, you need to buy the Hauler ($1.50M), go to LSIA upgrade station, upgrade it to the Hauler Custom (5.00M), spawn the Hauler Custom (hauler2), go to LSIA upgrade station, and pay $10 Billion ($10,000,000,000). The Chernobog Toter has a base carry capacity of 6000kg (6900kg with Premium).
