CollinsCo Cabbies


As a CollinsCo Cabbie you'll take on the role of a taxi driver. Not just any taxi driver however, you'll be expected to be well on time. In this job you will be rewarded with money and Business Experience.

Getting Started

  • Go to the Cabbie Depot CocoCabbieDepot blip. On the east side of the parking lot is a door with an orange job marker, Choose CollinsCo Cabbies.
  • Head over to the stairs in the northern part of the lot, go on duty at the blue marker.
  • Find any vehicle that can carry at least one passenger. You can use your own vehicles.
  • Your status is set as available, drive around, looking for a passenger CocoFriend 22px.
    • A VIP passenger is guaranteed to spawn if the Idle Cycles reach 30/30.
  • Horn (default E) to alert your passenger. A GPS route will guide you to the destination CocoDestination 23px.
  • Drive to the destination.
  • Upon dropping off the passenger, you'll receive money and Business Experience.
    • Arriving within the time bonus will increase the amount of money (?) and Experience earned.

Streak

As you complete Cabbie callouts, you increase your streak by 1, this will increase the amount of exp you gain per dropoff.

The Cabbies streak starts at 6 exp. The exp for a dropoff is dependent on the distance of the delivery, the current streak and if you manage to deliver in time.

The formula for raw exp: (distance) * ((100 + streak)/100) * (time bonus)

Where distance can have the value 6 exp for deliveries that are less than 3500m away and 7.5 exp for ones that are further away and the time bonus is a *2 Multiplier if you manage to deliver in time.

Roxwood CollinsCo Cabbie

There is a new starting point for CoCo Cabbie, you can find this on the map in Roxwood. You need to go to the depot and enter to change job to CollinsCo Cabbie, you can spawn a cabbie at the depot or bring your own 2 seater vehicle. Then follow all the steps above after you have got the job and gone on duty.
