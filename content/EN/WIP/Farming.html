Farming is one of the Jobs (and skills) available in Transport Tycoon. As a Farmer, you are responsible for harvesting and processing crops. You have the choice of Farming in Paleto Bay,Grapeseed or Roxwood. All areas have multiple fields that can be bought provided that you have the level for the field. With the crops you harvest, you can feed livestock such as chickens, pigs and cows.

Getting Started

First, you will need to visit any Job Center (except for Life Invader) and choose the Farmer job. Once you have the Farmer job, you'll need a tractor that you can buy from the Car Dealership or find a randomly spawned one. As a beginner Farmer, you only have access to a small field at Paleto bay, so go there and buy it (30.000$) .
Every field is locked behind a specific Farming level, locked fields show up as (see Fields).

Obtaining seeds

  • The first 3 types of seeds are available at the Market (Farmer Shop)[1], there are 2 locations on the map, 1 at Paleto and 1 at Grapeseed . If you are part of a Faction, they can also purchase 1 Farmers' Market.
  • To obtain others seeds you will need to get them through other means, such as Prospecting, opening Chests, trading with other people or obtaining them through the Marketplace.
  1. The Farmers market uses a generic Market icon that is used for different types of markets. Make sure to check the name of the blip through the map legend to find a correct market type.

Cultivating

There are multiple steps from cultivating the fields to eventually harvesting the crops.

  • First, you'll need a tractor that you can buy from the Car Dealership or find a randomly spawned one.
  • After that go to an attachment point and select "Tractor (Plough)".
  • Next, go to the field you bought previously and start the job by driving on the field. Once you've completed the area, you will be awarded with Farming Experience.
  • Now go back to the attachment point and select "Tractor (Seeder)". Once you get back to the field, a menu will open to choose your seed to be planted. Every seed has a specific level requirement and growth time[1] (see Seeds). You start with carrot seeds and unlock other seeds later. You now once again drive over the field until the area is completed. You now have to wait for the seeds to fully grow[1].
  • If You have a Fertilizer (obtained from a Farming Perk or Marketplace), You can visit the attachment point to select "Tractor (Fertilizer)" and visit your field once again to apply a fertilizer to it. This will half the growth time.
  • You will see a small green circle around your field blip when growing is finished .
    • You can check the progress of a field through your map. Select the Farming field blip on your map and an infobox will display the time remaining[1].
  • Go the the attachment point (again) and select "Tractor (Harvester)" and once again ride on your field until the area is completed. For harvesting, you will be rewarded with Farming experience and the raw Farming items.

Worker Tokens

Obtaining Worker Tokens

Worker tokens are obtained through completing tasks on fields such as Cultivating, Planting, Fertilizing and Harvesting Your Fields and are given by chance. Worker tokens can also be obtained by purchasing from other players through the Marketplace or trading together.

Using Worker Tokens

Worker tokens can be used at Farm Workers and instantly completes tasks on fields like Cultivating, Planting and Harvesting. (XP is only granted when Harvesting, and no xp is given when Cultivating or Planting). 1 Worker Token completes 1 Task. The Farm Workers is located in Grapeseed at the O'Neil farm house on the north east side.

Animal breeding

The second aspect of the farmer job involves taking care of livestock. There are no level requirements, the only thing you need is money.

  • Head to a holding pen (red house) blip on the map and buy one of them. (you can buy them all at once if you are rich)
  • After buying it, a lot of information is displayed:
    • Animal Type
    • Total animals
    • Feed level
    • Next feed tick
    • Feed animals
    • Next collection time

All you have to do here is take care of your animals by feeding them regularly. At the start you'll only have 1 animal, but you can buy more of them by hitting the "Total animals" button (price is increasing after each animal bought).
Once you have animals on your farm, make sure to feed them as you will lose all but one of your animals if they do not have enough food! You can see a "feeding bar" which decreases by 1 tick each hour for every animal you have in that pen[1]. You can collect your reward every day (24 hours)[1], this will give you a generous amount of Farming Experience and some items related to the type of pen you collected from.
