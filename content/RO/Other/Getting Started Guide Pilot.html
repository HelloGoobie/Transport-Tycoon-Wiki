Bine ați venit la Transport Tycoon

Transport Tycoon se referă la transportul de mărfuri, oameni și materiale peste San Andreas în camioane, avioane, mașini și multe altele! În timp ce faceți acest lucru, veți avea ocazia să cumpărați mașini, a case, haine, sau chiar cumpărare Afaceri și obțineți venituri reziduale.

Level up your careers; you can be a Pilot, a Truck driver, you can deliver mail or even become an EMS and help people--AI and players alike! There are many jobs to choose from!

Pilot de avion este unul dintre joburile (și abilitățile) disponibile în Transport Tycoon. În calitate de pilot al companiei aeriene, veți transporta pasageri către și de la diferite aeroporturi. Va trebui să porniți cu un avion mic și să vă pregătiți treptat pentru a prelua controlul liniilor aeriene uriașe. Ca orice loc de muncă egal, salariul dvs. va depinde foarte mult de progresul pe care îl aveți.

Getting Started; Airline Pilot Job and Requirements

ATC is mandatory, make sure to fully read this guide before flying. Failure to follow ATC can result in punishment leading up to bans.

  • To begin this job, as with most other jobs, you need to visit a Job Center or have the appropriate job card and change your job to Airline Pilot.
  • To buy your first plane, you will need to visit the LSIA Aircraft shop , located on the north-western side of the airport behind the Los Santos Customs.
  • Your First plane will be the Vestra which Costs you about $20.000
  • As you only have $10.000 you need to get another 10.000$ so you could:
    • Do another Job first like Trucker, UPS Delivery or Fisher.
    • Go to the Fountain of Wealth located at the Legion Square in Los Santos.
  • Once you have bought your plane you will need to taxi to a terminal . At the terminal, you will be prompted to press "E" to let your passengers board the plane. Make sure to not drift outside of the boarding zone or you will be punished with a 10-minute cooldown.
  • After all the passengers have boarded you will now see a lighter terminal icon.
  • Fly to that airport (remember to use ATC) and deboard your passengers at the marked gate.
  • After your passengers have been deboarded successfully, you will be rewarded with money and airline vouchers, it will then automatically board new passengers that need to be transported to another airport.
  • Vouchers can be redeemed at the Grand Exchange for XP.
  • For a more in-depth look at airline piloting, head over to the Airline Pilot page. Here you will find more info on other planes you can buy as you rank up, the use of atc, airport names and locations and the difference between light and heavy aircraft.
